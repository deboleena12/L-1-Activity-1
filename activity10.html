Building a healthy plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A healthy plate gives our body the energy it needs to study,play,think, and grow. It should include colourful fruits, vegetables, grains, and protein-rich food.

Smart Snack Choices

Snacks can be healthy when we choose foods that give energy instead of only sugar. Fruits,nuts,sprouts, and yoghurt are better choice than chips and sugary drinks.

Nutriton Tips for Students

Drink enough water

Water helps the body stay active, supports digestion, and keeps the brain focused.Students should carry a water bottle and drink throughout the day.

Eat colourful food

Different colours in fruits and vegetables give different nutrients. A colourful plate often means healthier plate
